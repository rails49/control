# Documentation

## Style

* Avoid overly technical language unless required for accurate description.
* Avoid embellishments and judgements, e.g. "the deep module and the research core".
* Keep short and to the point without loosing accuracy.

## Em-dashes: measured, not overlooked

This list used to ask writers to avoid overusing em-dashes. It no longer does,
by decision.

The density was counted twice. #163 swept ten of them out of the `#151`/`#155`
range and landed; the very next batch, `df2581b..1c226f5`, added about 270 over
some 7,000 added lines. A rule that survives a sweep and comes back at that
volume is not being broken by accident — it describes prose nobody writes here,
including the prose that passes review on every other axis. #178 raised it a
second time and #184 accepted the register and deleted the rule. Do not file it
a third time.
